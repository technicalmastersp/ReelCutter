Privacy policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026. This page explains what happens to your data when you use Reel Cutter.

The short version

Your video files are processed entirely inside your own browser tab. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers, because Reel Cutter doesn't have a server that handles video at all. What follows are the details, including how advertising on this site works.

1. Video files

When you choose a video in the split tool, it is read directly by JavaScript running on your device and processed in your browser's memory. It is not sent to Reel Cutter, to any cloud storage, or to any third party. When you close the tab, the file and any generated parts are discarded unless you've downloaded them yourself.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not require an account, do not collect names or email addresses to use the tool, and do not log the contents, filenames, or metadata of the videos you process.

3. Cookies and advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. We do not control these technologies directly; they are governed by Google's own policies.

  • You can review how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings, or via aboutads.info/choices for participating networks generally.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand aggregate traffic to this site (for example, which pages are visited and roughly how often), separate from any video processing. This data does not include the contents of any video you split.

5. Children's privacy

Reel Cutter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

Because no account or file data is stored on our end, there is generally nothing on our servers for you to request or delete. For questions about advertising cookies specifically, use the opt-out links above or your browser's cookie/tracking-protection settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or advertising setup changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.

This policy is provided for general information and is not legal advice. If you operate this site commercially, have it reviewed by a qualified professional for your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) before publishing.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, ideas?

Reel Cutter is a small, ad-supported tool. A proper contact form is on its way — for now, here's where to look for the kind of help you need.

Something not working?

If a video fails to split, please note the file format (MP4/MOV), roughly how it was recorded or exported, and the exact error text from the log box on the split tool page — that's usually enough to diagnose a container quirk.

Feature requests

Suggestions for new settings, formats, or export options are welcome. Reel Cutter intentionally stays a single-purpose splitter rather than a full editor, but reasonable improvements to the splitting workflow are fair game.

Advertising, press, or legal

For anything related to advertising on this site, press inquiries, or legal/privacy matters, use the contact form note below.

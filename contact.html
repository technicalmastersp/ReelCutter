Get in touch

Questions, bugs, ideas?

Reel Cutter is a small, ad-supported tool with no live support team, but real people read every message.

Something not working?

If a video fails to split, please include the file format (MP4/MOV), roughly how it was recorded or exported, and the exact error text from the log box on the split tool page. That's usually enough to diagnose a container quirk.

Email: support@reelcutter.app

Feature requests

Suggestions for new settings, formats, or export options are welcome. Reel Cutter intentionally stays a single-purpose splitter rather than a full editor, but reasonable improvements to the splitting workflow are fair game.

Email: hello@reelcutter.app

Advertising, press, or legal

For anything related to advertising on this site, press inquiries, or legal/privacy matters, use the address below and mention the topic in your subject line.

Email: legal@reelcutter.app

Reel Cutter never asks for your video files by email — no attachment support is needed since the tool never uploads footage in the first place.

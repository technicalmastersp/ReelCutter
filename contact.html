Get in touch

Questions, bugs, ideas?

Reel Cutter is a small, ad-supported tool. Email is the fastest way to reach us — here's where to look for the kind of help you need.

Email

For support, bug reports, feedback, advertising or press inquiries, or anything about privacy/terms — email us directly:

technicalmastersp@gmail.com

Something not working?

If a video fails to split, check our troubleshooting guide first — it covers the most common errors (parsing failures, browser crashes, audio issues) with source-specific fixes for phones, screen recorders, and editors, and resolves most cases without needing to wait on a reply.

If that doesn't cover it, email us with a few details that make diagnosis much faster:

  • The file format (MP4/MOV) and roughly how it was recorded or exported (which app, device, or camera).
  • The approximate length and resolution of the source file.
  • The exact error text from the log box on the split tool page.
  • Which browser and device you're using (e.g. "Safari on iPhone," "Chrome on Windows laptop").

Feature requests

Suggestions for new settings, formats, or export options are welcome. Reel Cutter intentionally stays a single-purpose splitter rather than a full editor, but reasonable improvements to the splitting workflow are fair game — several past changes (including support for fragmented MP4 files) came directly from user reports.

Response times

Reel Cutter is maintained independently rather than by a support team, so replies aren't instant — but every message is read, and bug reports with the details above are usually the fastest to get a fix. For general questions, the About page and tutorials answer most common ones already.

Reel Cutter never needs your video files to help with support — the tool never uploads footage in the first place, so there's nothing to attach.
